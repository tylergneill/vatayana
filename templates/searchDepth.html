More info

The system compares documents using fast topic modeling, slower TF-IDF, and slowest Smith-Waterman methods. The sliders here control how many comparisons occur in the latter two steps, letting you modify the balance between speed and quality.

Top similarity scores from TF-IDF and Smith-Waterman are saved to speed up future searches. By default, up to 500 TF-IDF and 25 Smith-Waterman results are stored, increasing over time to limits of 4000 and 200 as users initiate searches that uncover new results. Choosing values within these limits contributes to faster results for everyone later; searches performed with higher values are processed and shown but not saved.

Time estimates apply to one docExplore query. docCompare takes roughly twice as long.
